About

I'm a mechanical engineer by training who spent 25+ years bridging technology and entertainment, including a stint as Head of Digital Media at NTV. I'm the author of Directing Business: Movies and Management Lessons for Dreamers.

These days I work out of Hyderabad, building with Claude as a member of the Claude Partner Network. Ideas get a quick build, then a sprint of two weeks at most, and the ones that hold up end up here.

I also run Directing Business Consulting & Advisory, a vendor-neutral AI strategy practice.
